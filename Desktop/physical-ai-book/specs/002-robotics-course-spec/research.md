# Research Plan: Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Course Book

## Research Areas

### 1. Robotics Technologies

- **ROS 2**: Best practices, common pitfalls, and comparison with ROS 1 and other middleware.
- **Gazebo & Unity**: Strengths and weaknesses of each for robotics simulation, and how they integrate with ROS 2.
- **NVIDIA Isaac**: In-depth review of Isaac Sim for perception and manipulation, and its advantages over other simulators.
- **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models**: Survey of current SOTA VLA models and how they can be integrated with ROS 2 for robotics tasks.

### 2. Hardware Specifications

- **Workstations**: Detailed specifications for RTX workstations suitable for robotics development and simulation.
- **Embedded Systems**: Comparison of different Jetson kits (e.g., Nano, AGX Orin) for on-robot deployment.
- **Sensors**: Evaluation of RealSense cameras and other common robotics sensors.
- **Robots**: Analysis of different robot platforms suitable for the capstone project, with a focus on affordability and capability.

### 3. Cloud vs. On-Premise

- **Cost Analysis**: A detailed cost comparison of on-premise hardware vs. using cloud services like AWS RoboMaker or NVIDIA Isaac Sim on AWS.
- **Performance**: Benchmarking the performance of simulation and training in the cloud vs. on a local workstation.
- **Latency**: Investigating the impact of network latency on cloud-based robotics development.

## Methodology

- **Literature Review**: Review academic papers, industry publications, and official documentation.
- **Hands-on Testing**: Where possible, perform hands-on tests of the technologies and hardware.
- **Expert Consultation**: Consult with experts in the field of robotics and AI.
